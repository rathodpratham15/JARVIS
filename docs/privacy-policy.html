J.A.R.V.I.S. — Personal AI Operating System

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 27, 2026

Short version: JARVIS is a self-hosted AI assistant. Your data is processed by the third-party AI APIs you configure (OpenAI, Google Gemini, ElevenLabs, etc.) and stored on your own backend. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

1. Who We Are

JARVIS – AI Assistant ("JARVIS", "the App", "we") is an open-source personal AI operating system developed by Pratham Rathod. The App connects your mobile device to a backend server that you control (self-hosted or deployed via Railway). Questions can be directed to rathod.pr@northeastern.edu.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the App's functionality:

Data typePurposeStored where
Account credentials (email, hashed password) Authentication Your backend database
Chat messages AI response generation Your backend; forwarded to AI API (OpenAI / Gemini)
Voice audio Speech-to-text transcription Processed in memory; not persisted beyond the request
Camera images / video frames Face recognition, scene analysis, OSINT Processed in memory; face embeddings stored on your backend if enrolled
Enrolled face photos Biometric recognition of people you add Your backend database (opt-in, you control who is enrolled)
Task, schedule, reminder data Agent and productivity features Your backend database
Research queries Web research via Tavily / Exa Forwarded to search API; not persisted by JARVIS

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or device telemetry.

3. Third-Party AI Services

JARVIS routes requests to third-party AI providers depending on your configuration. Each provider has its own privacy policy:

  • OpenAI — chat, agents, STT  openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Google Gemini / Vertex AI — vision, chat  policies.google.com/privacy
  • Groq — fast inference  groq.com/privacy-policy
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech  elevenlabs.io/privacy
  • Tavily / Exa — web search for research  tavily.com/privacy

You control which providers are active by configuring API keys on your backend. Data sent to these providers is governed by their terms, not ours.

4. Camera and Microphone

The App requests camera and microphone permissions only when you actively use Vision Mode or Voice Mode respectively. Camera frames and audio are sent to your backend over HTTPS for processing. We do not record or store raw audio or video on device beyond the current session.

Face enrollment is strictly opt-in. You explicitly add people by name and photo. You can delete any enrolled identity at any time from the Vision screen.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for advertising or commercial purposes.

Data is shared only in these circumstances:

  • With the AI APIs listed in Section 3, solely to fulfil your request.
  • If required by law or to protect legal rights.

6. Data Retention

Because JARVIS is self-hosted, you control retention. Chat history, tasks, schedules, and enrolled faces are stored in your database and can be deleted at any time. We have no access to your database and cannot recover data on your behalf.

7. Security

All communication between the App and your backend is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage. API keys are stored server-side only and never sent to the mobile client.

8. Children's Privacy

JARVIS is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (by uninstalling the App and clearing your backend database)
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (EU/UK users)

Because JARVIS is self-hosted, exercising these rights is typically as simple as deleting records from your own database. For any further requests contact rathod.pr@northeastern.edu.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be noted in the App's release notes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
